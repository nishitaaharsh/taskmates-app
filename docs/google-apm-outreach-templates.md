# Google APM Outreach Email Templates

**Target role:** Associate Product Manager, University Graduate, 2027 Start

## How to use these templates

Replace the bracketed fields before sending. Keep the final message under 150 words when possible. Do not describe anyone as the hiring manager unless they confirm that responsibility. The public research found no exact professional email addresses, so these drafts are designed primarily for LinkedIn messages or verified Google recruiting channels.

Use one thoughtful, role-specific reference rather than mentioning several unrelated details. Attach a one-page resume only when the recipient invites it or when the recruiting channel permits attachments.

## Outreach guardrails

Do not send the same message to all 30 people. Start with five to seven high-relevance contacts and personalize each note. Avoid claiming that a person is the hiring manager unless they explicitly say so. Do not ask a senior executive directly for an interview or referral in the first message. Ask for a small, answerable piece of advice. Finally, use the live Google Careers posting and official APM program page when referencing deadlines or application requirements, because the supplied role text contains inconsistent cohort dates.
